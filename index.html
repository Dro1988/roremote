RoRemote

A Roku + Android TV remote for Android that works over your Wi-Fi. Controls Roku AND Android TV / Google TV boxes (onn and others). Auto-discovery, full D-pad remote, keyboard, and a Roku app launcher.

Download RoRemote v1.6 (APK)

Android 8.0+. Phone and TV/box must be on the same Wi-Fi. Android TV pairs once with a code shown on the TV.
When installing, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser if Android asks.
